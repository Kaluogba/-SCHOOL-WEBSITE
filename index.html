Platform Features

Student Registration

Easy sign-up capturing student and parent/guardian details including WhatsApp number for automated updates.

Payment Tracker

Secure fee payment tracking with automatic learning path assignment upon payment verification.

Curated Video Lessons

Access to subject-specific YouTube videos within your assigned learning path. Time spent is tracked.

Randomized CBT Quizzes

After watching a video, you get a randomly generated quiz to test your understanding. Must pass to continue.

Q&A Chat

Ask questions about the video and get answers from instructors in real time.

Parent Notifications

Automatic feedback messages sent to parents/guardians' WhatsApp after activities.

AI Student Manager

AI analyzes your performance, time on videos, quiz scores, and suggests personalized learning improvements.

Progress Dashboard

Students and parents can monitor learning progress, video completion, quiz scores, and AI recommendations.
