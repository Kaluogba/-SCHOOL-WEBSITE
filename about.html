About KIE

Knowledge Intelligence Excellence (KIE) is an innovative educational platform designed to help students learn at their own pace while keeping parents informed. We combine curated video lessons, randomized CBT quizzes, AI-powered learning recommendations, and real-time progress tracking into a single seamless experience.

Our Mission

To democratize quality education by leveraging technology to make learning accessible, engaging, and measurable. We believe every student has the potential to excel when given the right tools and guidance.

Our Approach

Students register, pay a fee, and gain access to subject-specific learning paths aligned to their assigned class. Each path contains curated YouTube video lessons. After watching a video, students must spend sufficient time on the topic (tracked at a minimum of the video duration), then take a randomly-generated CBT quiz. All feedback is sent automatically to the parent's WhatsApp. Our AI Student Manager studies each learner's patterns and suggests personalized improvement strategies.

Key Features

  • Video-based learning with watch-time tracking
  • Randomized CBT quizzes after each lesson
  • AI-powered personalized learning recommendations
  • Real-time WhatsApp notifications to parents
  • Instructor chat for questions and answers
  • Payment tracking with automatic course unlocking
  • Progress dashboard for students and parents

The Team

KIE is built by educators and technologists who understand the Nigerian education landscape. Our curriculum is aligned with the WAEC and JAMB syllabi, ensuring relevance to exam preparation.
